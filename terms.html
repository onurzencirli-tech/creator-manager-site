What the service is

Creator Manager is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner connect their own social media accounts and publish their own content to them.

Who may use it

Creator Manager is used only by its owner for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Connected accounts

When an account is connected, Creator Manager acts only on the owner's instruction and only within the permissions the owner grants on the platform's own consent screen. The owner can disconnect an account at any time from the application or from the platform's account settings.

Platform rules

Content published through Creator Manager is subject to the terms and community guidelines of the platform it is published to, including the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
