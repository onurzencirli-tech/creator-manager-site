Creator Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Data we access

When the owner connects an account, Creator Manager receives only what the owner approves on the platform's consent screen. For TikTok this is basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account. For Instagram this is the connected professional account's basic profile, permission to publish to it and to read its insights and, when the comment feature is used, comments on that account's own posts and messages sent to that account.

Comments and messages on Instagram

When a Reel invites viewers to comment a keyword to receive a resource, Creator Manager reads comments on the owner's own posts to find those requests. It replies to that comment, sends the commenter the requested link in a private message, and checks whether the commenter follows the account to decide which message to send. It messages only people who commented or wrote to the account first. It stores the commenter's username, the comment ID, the delivery status and the time, so that nobody is contacted twice. It does not store the text of messages.

Where data is stored

Access tokens, account details and the comment records above are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Creator Manager; there is none. This website is static and stores nothing.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the connected account in the application and to publish content the owner chooses to publish. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in Creator Manager deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; on Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites). If you commented on one of our posts and want your record deleted, contact us below and it will be removed.
